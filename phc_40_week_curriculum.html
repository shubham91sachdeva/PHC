PROGRESSIVE HEALTH CLUB · 40-WEEK SESSION PLAN · v1.0

40-Week Interactive Curriculum

One session, one week, 40 weeks per academic year — mapped for two categories. Every single session is built around an activity, not a lecture: experiments, games, role-play, debate, simulation, and workshops. Students do something in every session; they are never just told something.

Format Legend 🧪 Experiment 🎲 Game 🎭 Role-Play 🗣️ Debate 🛠️ Workshop 🎯 Simulation 🌳 Outdoor 📋 Project ⭕ Circle Discussion 👩‍⚕️ Expert Session 🎨 Creative/Art ✍️ Journaling
🍬 Flagship Recurring Project — The Class Sugar Board

A growing wall display students build themselves: real food packets pinned up with their sugar, carb, fat and protein content laid bare — chocolates, chips, drinks, biscuits. It launches in the nutrition term and keeps growing all year, so "reading a label" becomes a habit, not a one-off lesson.

Weeks 1–10 · Hygiene, Dental, Nutrition & Movement

July – September
1

Welcome to PHC — Health Bingo

🎲 Game
Break the ice, introduce the 6 health dimensions, and set a personal health baseline — without a single lecture slide.
5 minHook: "Find someone who…" mixer (has 8 hrs sleep, eats breakfast daily, etc.)
25 minMain: Health Bingo card — students move around ticking boxes matching classmates to habits
10 minReflect: Each student writes 1 personal health goal for the year on an index card
Materials: Bingo cards, pens, goal cards
2

Germ Glitter — Handwashing Science

🧪 Experiment
Make the invisible visible — see exactly why soap beats water alone.
5 minHook: Guess how many germs are on your hands right now (write guesses)
25 minMain: Glitter = germs. Try removing with water only, then soap + 6-step technique. Compare.
10 minReflect: Class creates a "6-Step Handwash Song" together
Materials: Glitter, soap, water stations, towels
3

Head-to-Toe Hygiene Relay

🎲 Game
Cover bathing, hair, nails, feet, and clothes hygiene through team competition, not a checklist.
5 minHook: "Hygiene or Myth?" rapid-fire true/false round
25 minMain: Relay race — teams run to stations (hair/nails/feet/skin) and correctly demo or answer to score
10 minReflect: Fastest-improving team explains their winning habit to class
Materials: Station cards, props (comb, nail clipper, socks)
4

Plaque Attack — Dental Disclosure Lab

🧪 Experiment
See your own brushing gaps with a disclosing tablet and fix technique on the spot.
5 minHook: Brush normally as you always do (timed, no coaching)
25 minMain: Chew disclosing tablet, see pink missed-plaque zones in mirror, re-brush correctly, re-check
10 minReflect: Students mark their own "smile map" showing weak zones to focus on
Materials: Disclosing tablets, mirrors, toothbrushes, smile-map worksheet
5

Egg in Vinegar — Sugar & Cavities

🧪 Experiment
Watch acid dissolve enamel in real time and connect it directly to sugary food and drink choices.
5 minHook: Set up egg-in-vinegar jars (started previous week, revealed today)
20 minMain: Observe dissolved shell, discuss what this means for teeth exposed to sugar/acid daily
15 minMain 2: Sort snack picture-cards into "teeth friend" / "teeth enemy" piles as teams
Materials: Eggs, vinegar jars (prepped in advance), snack picture cards
6

🍬 Sugar Board Launch + Build-Your-Thali Challenge

📋 Project🎲 Game
Apply Rainbow Plate concepts through the thali game, then launch the year-long Class Sugar Board.
5 minHook: Rainbow Plate quick-recap quiz (buzzer style)
20 minMain: Teams get food picture-cards and 90 seconds to build the most balanced thali; judged and scored
15 minLaunch: Empty wall board unveiled — each student brings one empty food packet from home next week to pin up; board sectioned into "Sugar", "Fat", "Protein", "Hidden Surprises"
Materials: Food picture cards, plate templates, blank classroom board, coloured section headers
7

Protein Power Sorting Relay

🎲 Game
Identify high-protein Indian foods and understand daily protein needs through movement, not memorisation.
5 minHook: "How much protein is in this?" guessing game with real packaging
25 minMain: Relay — run, grab a food card, sort into "protein hero" or "not really" bin, tag team
10 minReflect: Start the Protein Meter tracker — first entry in class together
Materials: Food cards, sorting bins, PHC Protein Meter worksheet
8

🍬 Sugar Board Update — Label Detective Scavenger Hunt

📋 Project🎲 Game
Pin every student's brought-in food packet to the Sugar Board with its real sugar/fat/carb count worked out live.
5 minHook: "This or that" — hold up two snacks, guess which has more sugar
25 minMain: In teams, examine each student's brought packet, calculate sugar/fat-per-serving, write it on a card and pin the packet + card onto the correct Sugar Board section
10 minReflect: Board reveal walk-through — "which 3 pins shocked you most?" — plus each student picks one swap for a healthier snack
Materials: Real snack packets/wrappers (student-sourced), Sugar Board, index cards, pins
9

Traditional Games Day

🌳 Outdoor
Experience movement as joy — kabaddi, kho-kho, lagori — while connecting fun to fitness science.
10 minHook: Quick warm-up + dynamic stretch circle led by a student
30 minMain: Rotate through 2–3 traditional games in small groups
5 minReflect: Cool-down stretch + "how did your body feel?" quick share
Materials: Playground/field, game markers
10

Fitness Circuit Stations

🛠️ Workshop
Learn the 5 components of fitness by physically rotating through stations that test each one.
5 minHook: Fitness trainer demos each of 5 stations (30 sec each)
28 minMain: 5 stations × 5 min each — cardio, strength, endurance, flexibility, balance
7 minReflect: Students record their favourite + hardest station on their fitness card
Materials: Cones, mats, timer, fitness trainer
Expert: Fitness Trainer

Weeks 11–20 · My Body, Growing Up & Safety

October – November
11

Body Systems Quiz Relay

🎲 Game
Learn organs and their jobs through a competitive relay, not a diagram to copy.
5 minHook: Silhouette poster — students stick organ cut-outs where they think organs go
25 minMain: Relay quiz — run, answer an organ-function question, tag next teammate
10 minReflect: Correct the silhouette poster together as a class
Materials: Body silhouette poster, organ cut-outs, quiz cards
12

My Body, My Rules — Boundaries Workshop

🎭 Role-Play
Introduce body autonomy and private-parts language before the deeper safety session next week.
10 minHook: "My body belongs to me" call-and-response chant
25 minMain: Small-group role-play — practising saying "no" confidently to uncomfortable requests (non-touch scenarios)
5 minReflect: Each student names their 3 trusted adults on a card to keep
Materials: Scenario cards, trusted-adult cards
13

Good Touch Bad Touch — Puppet Theatre

🎭 Role-Play
Deliver the most sensitive topic of the year through puppets so it's approachable, not frightening.
10 minHook: Puppet show sets up 3 everyday scenarios
20 minMain: Class calls out "Good Touch!" / "Bad Touch!" / "Confusing — still tell!" as puppets act; discuss each
10 minReflect: Practice the "Say No, Go, Tell" rule out loud together 3 times
Materials: Puppets, scenario script
Expert: Child Psychologist (co-facilitates)
14

Growing Up — What's Happening to My Body

⭕ Circle Discussion
Normalise puberty changes through an anonymous question box format — separate groups by gender.
15 minHook: Anonymous question box — students write any question, no names, drop in box
20 minMain: Facilitator answers questions live in circle — timeline of changes, "all bodies are different timelines"
5 minReflect: "One thing I learned today" — quick popcorn share
Materials: Question box, slips of paper
Expert: Paediatrician (gender-separated groups)
15

Menstrual Health & Pee Safe Kit Day

👩‍⚕️ Expert Session
Girls get full menstrual hygiene education and their Pee Safe kit; boys get a parallel allyship session.
15 minGirls: Product demo — pad/cup basics, hands-on with samples
15 minGirls: Kit distribution + "ask anything" open floor
30 minBoys (parallel): "Being a good friend" discussion + myth-busting quiz
Materials: Pee Safe sanitary kits, product samples
Expert: Gynaecologist + Pee Safe representative
16

Stranger Danger 2.0 — Online Safety Sim

🎯 Simulation
Apply safety rules to online scenarios through realistic simulated chat exchanges.
5 minHook: "Would you tell a stranger this in real life?" rapid quiz
25 minMain: Mock chat scripts projected — class votes "safe reply" vs "red flag" at each turn
10 minReflect: Build a class list of "never share online" items together
Materials: Printed/projected mock-chat scripts
17

Feelings Charades

🎲 Game
Build emotional vocabulary through acting and guessing, not a worksheet of feeling words.
5 minHook: Feelings Wheel intro — 8 core emotions
25 minMain: Charades — act out an emotion + a body sensation, class guesses both
10 minReflect: "A time I felt this way" — pair-share with a partner
Materials: Feelings Wheel poster, emotion cards
18

Calm-Down Toolkit Workshop

🛠️ Workshop
Build and practise 4 physical stress-relief tools students can use independently.
5 minHook: "What does stress feel like in YOUR body?" quick body-map drawing
25 minMain: 4 stations — balloon breathing, counting-to-10, squeeze-ball, draw-it-out — rotate through all
10 minReflect: Students pick their favourite tool and write it on a bracelet/wristband card to keep
Materials: Stress balls, paper, colours, station cards
19

Walk in My Shoes — Empathy Lab

🎭 Role-Play
Build empathy by physically swapping perspectives in guided scenarios.
5 minHook: Pass-the-shoe game — literally swap a shoe and share a "hard day" story
25 minMain: Scenario cards — pairs act out a conflict from both perspectives, swapping roles halfway
10 minReflect: "What surprised you about the other side?" circle share
Materials: Scenario cards
20

Trust Fall Friendship Challenge

🌳 Outdoor
Build team trust and identify qualities of a healthy friendship through cooperative physical games.
10 minHook: Human knot game — untangle as a team
20 minMain: Trust-walk (blindfolded, partner-guided obstacle course)
10 minReflect: "3 qualities of a friend I trust" — sticky notes on a class board
Materials: Blindfolds, cones, sticky notes

Weeks 21–30 · Social Health, Digital Wellness & Yoga

December – January
21

Peer Pressure — Choose Your Ending

🎭 Role-Play
Practise saying no to peer pressure through branching-choice skits.
5 minHook: "Would you?" rapid scenarios, quick hands-up votes
25 minMain: Small groups act out a peer-pressure scene, freeze at the decision point, audience shouts alternate endings to try
10 minReflect: Build a class list of "exit lines" to use under pressure
Materials: Scenario cards
22

Bystander Power — Forum Theatre

🎭 Role-Play
Turn passive bystanders into active upstanders against bullying through interactive theatre.
10 minHook: Short bullying scene performed by trained student actors — ends unresolved
20 minMain: Audience members can shout "Stop!", step in, and replay the scene differently
10 minReflect: List concrete upstander actions on the board
Materials: Scripted scenario, no props needed
23

Kindness Chain Project — Launch

📋 Project
Turn kindness into an observable, trackable class-wide challenge.
10 minHook: Brainstorm 20 small acts of kindness as a class
20 minMain: Each student picks one to commit to this week; writes it on a paper chain link
10 minReflect: Chain goes up on the wall — grows each week as acts are completed
Materials: Coloured paper strips, stapler, wall space
24

Kindness Chain — Share Circle

⭕ Circle Discussion
Close the loop on the kindness project with reflection and celebration.
25 minMain: Circle share — each student tells the class the kind act they did and how it felt
10 minReflect: Vote on the "Kindness Story of the Week"
5 minWrap: Class photo with the completed kindness chain
Materials: Completed kindness chain
25

Screens: Friend or Foe? — Class Debate

🗣️ Debate
Explore screen time trade-offs through structured argument instead of a one-sided lecture.
10 minHook: Split class into "For" and "Against" teams — screens are good for kids
20 minMain: 2-minute opening arguments each side, rebuttals, audience questions
10 minReflect: Class co-creates a "balanced screen use" agreement
Materials: Debate prep cards
26

Spot the Scam — Online Safety Game

🎯 Simulation
Recognise online scams, fake profiles, and phishing attempts through realistic examples.
5 minHook: Show a real (anonymised) scam message — "who would fall for this?"
25 minMain: Teams sort a stack of message printouts into "Safe" / "Scam" / "Tell an Adult" piles, explain reasoning
10 minReflect: Build a class "red flag checklist" for online messages
Materials: Printed sample messages
27

My Digital Footprint — Paper Profile

🎨 Creative/Art
Visualise what a digital footprint looks like before ever posting for real.
10 minHook: "Everything you post is like writing in permanent marker" — quick demo with a marker on paper vs pencil
25 minMain: Students design a mock "profile page" on paper, then swap with a partner who circles anything unsafe to share
5 minReflect: Class list of "think before you post" rules
Materials: Profile template printouts, markers
28

Sun Salutation Practice

🛠️ Workshop
Learn the simplified 5-step Sun Salutation through guided practice, not theory.
5 minHook: Animal pose warm-up (Cat-Cow, Downward Dog stories)
25 minMain: Guided 5-step Sun Salutation practice, 3 rounds, breath-aligned
10 minReflect: 3-minute guided relaxation (mini Yoga Nidra) to close
Materials: Yoga mats/floor space
Expert: Yoga Instructor
29

Balloon Breath & Bumble Bee Breath

🛠️ Workshop
Practise two pranayama techniques for calming and energising, hands-on.
5 minHook: "How does your breath change when you're scared vs calm?" quick discussion
25 minMain: Guided practice — Balloon Breath (belly), then Bhramari/Bumble Bee (humming exhale), 5 rounds each
10 minReflect: Journal — "which breath will I use when I feel anxious before a test?"
Materials: Journals
Expert: Yoga Instructor
30

Stress Relief Station Rotation

🛠️ Workshop
Consolidate a full toolkit — breathing, movement, journaling, and yoga — into one practised routine.
5 minHook: Recap — name every stress tool learned this term
25 minMain: 4 stations (breathing corner, stretch corner, journal corner, quiet art corner) — rotate 6 min each
10 minReflect: Students build a personal "my calm-down plan" card to keep in their bag
Materials: Station props, personal cards

Weeks 31–40 · Values, Money, Environment & Celebration

February – April
31

The Honesty Dilemma — Story Circle

⭕ Circle Discussion
Explore integrity through relatable moral dilemmas, not preaching.
10 minHook: Short story stops at a decision point — "What should she do?"
20 minMain: Small groups debate and vote on the ending, then compare with the real story's outcome
10 minReflect: "A time I chose honesty even when it was hard" — voluntary share
Materials: Story cards
32

Fairness Court — Mini Mock Trial

🎯 Simulation
Practise ethical reasoning and accountability through a playful class "courtroom."
5 minHook: Set the scene — a fictional classroom "unfairness" case
25 minMain: Students play judge, jury, witnesses; argue both sides of a fairness dilemma
10 minReflect: Class verdict + discussion on what fairness actually means
Materials: Role cards, gavel prop (fun)
33

Class Economy — Money Simulation Launch

🎯 Simulation
Learn needs vs wants and saving by running a mini classroom economy over 2 weeks.
10 minHook: Introduce PHC-dollars — students "earn" them for tasks this week
20 minMain: Set up a mini "shop" of needs (pencils, stickers) vs wants (extra recess, prizes); students plan spend/save
10 minReflect: Predict — "will you save or spend this week?"
Materials: Play currency, shop items
34

Class Economy — Shop Day

🎯 Simulation
Experience the real consequence of saving vs impulsive spending.
25 minMain: Shop is open — students spend or save their earned PHC-dollars; savers get a small bonus
10 minReflect: "Who saved? Who spent it all? What would you do differently?" open discussion
5 minWrap: Introduce Save-Spend-Give jar concept for real pocket money
Materials: Play currency, shop items
35

Nature Walk & Pollution Scavenger Hunt

🌳 Outdoor
Connect environmental health to daily observation, not an abstract topic.
10 minHook: "What do healthy vs unhealthy environments look like?" quick brainstorm
25 minMain: Guided school-ground walk — spot and log signs of pollution, green spaces, water sources
10 minReflect: Map findings on a class chart; pick one issue to act on
Materials: Clipboards, observation sheets
36

Dirty Water Filter — Build Challenge

🧪 Experiment
Understand water quality and filtration by building a working filter.
10 minHook: Show a jar of visibly dirty water — "how would you clean this?"
25 minMain: Teams build a layered filter (sand, gravel, cloth, charcoal) in a bottle and test it
10 minReflect: Compare team results; discuss why we still boil/treat water even after filtering
Materials: Bottles, sand, gravel, charcoal, cloth, dirty water sample
37

Community Health Project — Planning

📋 Project
Design a small, real health-awareness action for the school or neighbourhood.
10 minHook: Brainstorm — "one health problem we could actually help fix nearby"
25 minMain: Teams pick a project (hygiene posters for younger classes, canteen suggestion drive, cleanup) and plan it
10 minReflect: Each team presents their one-sentence project pitch
Materials: Planning worksheets, chart paper
38

Health Report Card — Self-Assessment Workshop

🛠️ Workshop
Reflect on the year's growth using data students collected themselves, not a test.
10 minHook: Pull out July's "Health Baseline" card from Week 1 — compare to today
25 minMain: Students fill in their final Health Report Card — fitness, nutrition habits, sleep, stress tools learned
10 minReflect: Pair-share one thing that genuinely changed this year
Materials: Health Report Card forms
39

🍬 Sugar Board Finale + Activity Day Rehearsal

Turn the year-long Sugar Board into a school-wide exhibit booth and rehearse all Activity Day stations.
15 minMain: Sugar Board team sorts the year's pinned packets into a "Top 10 Shockers" mini-exhibit for the booth
15 minMain: Remaining teams rehearse their assigned booth/demo (yoga showcase, fitness challenge corner)
10 minReflect: Full run-through with peer feedback

Certification Day & Health Pledge

Make BMR/TDEE tangible by having students calculate their own using real formulas.
10 minHook: "How many calories do you burn just lying in bed all day?" guesses
